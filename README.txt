Run locally
-----------
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open this folder in VS Code.
3. Install/use the Live Server extension, then right-click index.html > Open with Live Server.
4. Open careers.html to test the most important page.

Important before publishing
---------------------------
- Role names and the Political Research Intern's Panchkula/on-site/3-month/₹10,000 per month details are based on the supplied screen recording.
- UI/UX, AI Engineering, Full Stack Engineering and Data Science roles are represented as six-month hybrid internships in Chandigarh; stipends are marked "To be confirmed" rather than invented.
- The source recording showed 30 October 2026 on the Data Science Intern page. Its meaning is marked for confirmation in the detail panel.
- Industry descriptions, principles, and marketing copy are draft positioning and should be approved by Sofsure.
- hello@sofsure.com and careers@sofsure.com are placeholder addresses. Replace with verified contacts.
- The application form is a UI prototype only. It does NOT send, store or upload applications. Connect a secure backend and privacy notice before accepting real applicant data.
- No candidate login/dashboard is included yet; this structure is intended to be a clean starting point for that later phase.
- The logo image is reused from the existing Sofsure prototype.
